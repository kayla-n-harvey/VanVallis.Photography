About Me

Hi, I'm Kayla!

I’ve had a camera in my hands since I was eleven years old. What started as a childhood fascination has grown into a lifelong passion for seeing the world through a lens.

Over the years, I’ve learned that the most powerful photographs aren't always the perfectly posed ones—they’re the ones that capture a feeling you never want to forget.

I decided to turn my love for photography into my career for a very personal reason: my family. As a mom of three kids and three dogs, life is beautiful, loud, and incredibly fast-moving.

I wanted a path that allowed me to be present for my own "special moments" while helping others freeze theirs in time.

Whether it’s a milestone celebration or a quiet, candid second between loved ones, I’m dedicated to capturing the authentic magic in your life.

I’d love to grab a coffee (or a treat for the pups!) and get to know you. Let’s chat about your vision and create something timeless together.
